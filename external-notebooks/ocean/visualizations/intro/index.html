Visualisation

Most of the datasets are part of one of the cubes, which in turn can be interactively visualised since they are in geozarr format. To open the visualisation click the “Explore Data” link in the ntoebooks, or go directly to the visualisation website . The site wraps a simple GeoZarr viewer and can be replaced with a compatible GeoZarr in any programming language or tool. Additionally, there are two notebooks if you are interested in converting your own data. They use a sample of the OceanSoda Dataset to demonstrate the conversions:

  2. Converting Zarr to GeoZarr
